## 实际 SQL（MyBatis DEBUG 摘录）

列表查询（参数 Q6871_full）：

```sql
SELECT p.person_id,p.name,...,
       f.floating_id,f.status floating_status,f.arrival_date,
       rp.permit_id,rp.permit_no masked_permit_no,rp.status permit_status,rp.valid_until permit_valid_until,
       CASE ... END last_migration_date,
       CASE ... END last_migration_direction
FROM person p
LEFT JOIN residence r ON r.person_id=p.person_id AND r.status='ACTIVE'
LEFT JOIN household h ON h.household_id=r.household_id
LEFT JOIN person hp ON hp.person_id=h.head_person_id
LEFT JOIN household_member hm ON hm.household_id=h.household_id AND hm.person_id=p.person_id AND hm.status='ACTIVE'
LEFT JOIN floating_population f ON f.person_id=p.person_id AND f.status='ACTIVE' AND f.current_flag=1
LEFT JOIN residence_permit rp ON rp.person_id=p.person_id AND rp.status='ACTIVE' AND rp.current_flag=1
LEFT JOIN (SELECT person_id,MAX(in_date) last_in_date FROM migration_in WHERE business_status='COMPLETED' GROUP BY person_id) latest_in ON latest_in.person_id=p.person_id
LEFT JOIN (SELECT person_id,MAX(out_date) last_out_date FROM migration_out WHERE business_status='COMPLETED' GROUP BY person_id) latest_out ON latest_out.person_id=p.person_id
WHERE p.name LIKE CONCAT('%',?,'%')
GROUP BY ...
ORDER BY p.person_id DESC LIMIT ? OFFSET ?
```

COUNT 查询使用同一 FROM/JOIN 与 WHERE，外层为 `COUNT(DISTINCT p.person_id)`；两处 GROUP BY 列包含全部非聚合投影并显式包含 latest_in.last_in_date/latest_out.last_out_date，MySQL 8.4 在 ONLY_FULL_GROUP_BY 下执行成功。

## EXPLAIN 观察

- `residence`：`uk_residence_person(person_id)` 被用作 eq_ref；实际索引清单含 `idx_residence_person_id`、`idx_residence_household_id`。
- `floating_population`：`uk_floating_person_current(person_id,current_flag)` 被用作 eq_ref；`idx_floating_region_status(current_region_code,status)` 存在。
- `residence_permit`：`uk_permit_person_current(person_id,current_flag)` 被用作 eq_ref；`idx_permit_region_status_expiry(issue_region_code,status,valid_until)` 存在。
- `migration_in/out`：派生聚合使用 `idx_migration_in_person_id` / `idx_migration_out_person_id` 的索引扫描（小数据集上的实际计划）；现有 `idx_migration_in_query(in_date,business_status,migration_type,person_id)` 与 `idx_migration_out_query` 保留。
- 列表主表 `p` 在 `name LIKE '%...%'`（前后通配）下为全表扫描，这是既有查询契约；未新增索引，也没有把该现象隐藏为“索引已优化”。
- 本轮未发现 SQL 语法错误、ONLY_FULL_GROUP_BY 错误、count 与列表不一致或分页重复；测试断言与日志见上。
